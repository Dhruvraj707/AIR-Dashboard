## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Camera Access
- When you open the app, your browser will ask for camera permission
- Click **"Allow"** to enable gesture drawing
- HTTPS is required (Streamlit provides this automatically)

### First Time Loading
- First deployment takes 3-5 minutes
- The app may "sleep" after 7 days of inactivity (free tier)
- Just visit the URL again to wake it up!

### If Something Goes Wrong
1. Check the **"Manage app"** page for error logs
2. Make sure your API key is correctly added in Secrets
3. Try restarting the app from the management page
